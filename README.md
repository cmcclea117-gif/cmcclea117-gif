## Hi, I'm CJ 👋

By day I work as a data engineer, DBA and BI developer. On evenings and weekends I run **PJ's Palace Games** with my family.

Our first game is **Wanderlands**, a journey card game for 2 to 6 players. You explore strange lands, find shrines and lore, and try to meet the pledges of your journey. It has a Story mode that grows chapter by chapter, smart AI explorers to play against, online games with friends, and no ads or tracking. I designed and built all of it, from the rules engine and game server to the art and trailers.

I grew up playing card games with my dad and grandma, and I wanted to make a game with that same feel: a few cards, a few good choices, and a journey you can read at a glance.
